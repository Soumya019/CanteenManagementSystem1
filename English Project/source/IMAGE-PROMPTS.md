# Image Generation Prompts — Six Plates

Paste each prompt into your image tool, download the result, and send the six PNGs back.

## Which tool

**Use Google's Nano Banana Pro (Gemini 3 Pro Image) in the Gemini app** for Plates I–VI.
These plates carry a lot of small text, and it is currently the strongest model at rendering
legible typography *inside* an image. In ChatGPT, use the image generation in GPT‑4o — it is
good, but expect more spelling retries on the label-heavy diagrams.

ratios need not be exact; the document scales by width.

## Before you send them to me

**Proofread the text in every image.** This is the one thing that can spoil an otherwise
beautiful plate. Check the dates (11 June 1894), the place names (Galesburg, Illinois;
Rue Montagne Sainte-Geneviève), and every heading against the prompt. If a word comes out
misspelled, stay in the same chat and say *"the label reads X, correct it to Y, change nothing
else"* — that usually fixes it in one pass.

If a plate is still garbled after two or three tries, send it anyway and tell me which one.
I can keep the AI artwork and lay sharp, correctly spelled text over it.

## House style — applies to Plates I, II, III, IV and VI

Every prompt below already contains this, but for reference, the six plates should look like
one set:

- Aged cream paper background, colour `#FBF7EF`
- Restrained palette only: **deep navy blue, black, brown, purple, sky blue**
- Elegant serif typography (Georgia / Liberation Serif character)
- The feel of a hand-inked mid-century editorial infographic, or a plate from a 1950s
  technical manual — engraved line work, fine rules, generous margins
- Flat and printed, not glossy: no 3‑D bevels, no drop shadows, no lens flare, no neon
- Entirely original artwork: no photographs, no film stills, no promotional material, no
  recognisable likeness of any real actor
